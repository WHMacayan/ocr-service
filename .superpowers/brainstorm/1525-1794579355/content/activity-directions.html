Activity: three ways to make it calm

All three answer "does anything need me?" first and push routine work out of the way. None of them changes what the service does, only how it's shown. Click the one that feels closest; mixing is fine.

A

Health on top, quiet history below

A short status strip says whether everything is running. Problems get their own list. The history underneath collapses routine work into one line per worker run, so hundreds of "wrote booking" lines become "Processed 14 emails, 31 Odoo writes". No rows flashing in; a small "5 new" button instead.

B

Two tabs: "Status" and "Full log"

Activity opens on Status, a calm overview that only changes when something changes. The Full log is a plain searchable table for looking things up. It shows your own actions and errors by default; routine service lines appear only when you switch them on.

C

Daily digest

Activity reads like a summary per day, newest first. Each day is one card: what came in, what went to Odoo, what broke. Problems are highlighted inside the day; everything else is folded and opens on a click. It reads like a report instead of a feed.

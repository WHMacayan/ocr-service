Emails as conversations: three layouts

In all three, the list shows conversations (not single emails), each labelled with its booking or bill. You can search by booking/bill number or just look at the newest. Sample data. Click the one that fits how you work.

A

Mail-client style: folders · conversations · thread

Like Outlook. Left: Bookings / Scraped bills and a "Needs attention" view. Middle: conversations. Right: the whole thread oldest-first; under each message, its attachments and what it wrote to Odoo.

B

Record first: each booking or bill is the "folder"

The list shows bookings and bills, each with its conversation folded inside. Opening one shows a short record summary on top (what's in Odoo), then the conversation that built it below. Best if you think "how is this booking going?" more than "what came in?".
